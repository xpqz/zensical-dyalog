# Plus `R←X+Y`

```apl
R←X+Y
```
[Key to notation](../key-to-notation.md)

`Y` must be numeric. `X` must be numeric. `R` is the arithmetic sum of `X` and `Y`. `R` is numeric. This function is also known as Add.

## Examples
```apl
      1 2 + 3 4
4 6
 
      1 2 + 3,⊂4 5
4  6 7
 
      1J1 2J2 + 3J3
4J4 5J5
 
      ¯5 + 4J4 5J5
¯1J4 0J5
```
